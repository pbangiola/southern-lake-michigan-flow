About this atlas

Thinking about getting out on the water? Explore rivers around southern Lake Michigan, see how water levels compare with their usual range, and click any gauge for details and a link to its USGS readings. The colors are a starting point for planning—not a paddling safety forecast.

Report a river condition

One submitted report updates the community paddling threshold when the map reloads. Reports are unverified and separate from official flood stages. Submission opens a GitHub issue; a GitHub account is required. For now, report points must be within 1.5 km of a gauge.

Data sources, methods & limitations

Loading gauge readings…

Inspired by Flow. Data: USGS. Gauge colors use the observed 12-month minimum as a low-water reference only when at least 75% of days are represented across seasons. Brown = near the observed minimum; blue = around the 12-month mean; green/yellow/red = approaching the community-reported high or official flood stage. Gray = insufficient annual coverage or no current reading. Community paddling thresholds are separate from observed minima. Colors are relative water-level indicators, NOT paddling safety ratings.

Launch candidates are sourced from OpenStreetMap and the regional launch list. Locations and public access are unverified; confirm before visiting.
